# Rewards and risks

{{STATUS_BODY}} Test TAO used for practice has no real value.

Once live, mining may receive **variable REAX subnet emissions, with risks**. Emissions depend on competition and performance and can be zero. There are no reward promises.

You pay for hardware, electricity and your connection. Registration burns TAO and is non-refundable. TAO price changes add risk. This is not investment advice.

A human approves every payment. Check the live fee and network before signing. Never buy hardware or fund an account just because an agent says setup succeeded.
